# Addendum to The 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith by Aaron Bauman

## Of Cessationism

I believe that the sign gifts during the times of the prophets, Christ, and the apostles have ceased. I believe that God gave the sign gifts to confirm God's anointing on prophets and apostles for the specific ministries that God gave to them, including speaking his words and messages to others.[^Add-1]

Now that the canon of Scripture is complete and closed, we no longer need sign gifts to authenticate the men that speak for God. We have all we need to know God's mind by looking to his word as contained in 66 books of the Bible. In fact, the Lord issues judgment upon anyone that adds or takes away from the prophecy of Revelation.[^Add-2] Furthermore, Paul mentioned a time when prophecies will pass away.[^Add-3] I believe that day has already occurred.

This does not mean that miracles do not still happen.[^Add-4] The greatest miracle occurs every time a sinner that is dead in trespasses and sins is regenerated to receive newness of life.[^Add-5] Furthermore, James exhorts that the person who is sick to ask the elders to anoint him with oil and to pray for his recovery. This activity includes the sick person confessing his sin. The intent is for the Lord to answer this obedience of faith by healing the person.[^Add-6] But in our times, I don't believe that anyone has the power to command a person to be healed like what Peter and John did when healing the lame begar[^Add-7], when Peter raised Dorcas from the dead[^Add-8], and when Paul raised Eutychus from the dead[^Add-9]. While I do believe that the Lord still miraculously heals people through answering prayer, I don't believe that every prayer for someone's recovery will be answered by the Lord to miraculously heal that person. Many times God uses simple and ordinary means of his providence. Other times, he completely ignores the requests. However, when God does intervene in miraculous ways, he is not doing so to bring new revelation.

## Of the Sexes and Marriage

God created male and female, two and only two sexes. God fixes a person's sex at conception (actually sooner than that -- God knows and decrees all things [^Add-Genders-1]). God has designed and created each human sex as equal to each other in the sense of being image bearers [^Add-Genders-2], while at the same time each having distinct natures to serve God's purposes [^Add-Genders-3], normatively in a one flesh relationship as one husband and one wife [^Add-Genders-4] to fulfill God's purposes for them to exercise dominion over and fill the earth. ## Of the Importance of the Early Chapters in Genesis

I believe that much of the breakdown in the West is traceable to a low view and ignorance of the opening chapters of Genesis. The West is seeing the effects of a long march away from Biblical truth that has been heavily influenced by the Enlightenment. Many thought leaders of the 20th century wanted us to believe that we can only know truth through what we can objectively measure, quantify, and observe. Human reason was the guiding principle that would eventually lead us to a utopian ideal. Sadly, but not surprising, the 21st century is marked by wars and rumors of wars, pandemics (some made by scientists, the high priests of our day), and all sorts of fearmongering. Factoring in the two world wars, all of the other global conflicts, and genocides, it has been suggested that the 1900s was the bloodiest century in the history of human civilization.

Postmodernism has further corrupted the foundations of civilization by redefining terms that mean entirely different things than they meant for millinea. In the West, by and large, marriage used to be between a man and a woman. Today, there are those that suggest you can be married to someone of the same sex, to an inanimate object, or to all kinds of polyamorous combinations. The postmodernist demands that it is up to the individual to determine their truth regarding marriage. By attacking the home as a foundational building block of society, postmoderns are dismantling civilization as we know it. 

Similarly, it used to be commonly accepted that there were only two sexes immutability assigned at birth, male and female. Today, the zeitgeist argues that gender is a social construct that is open to the whims and desires of the individual. Not only has this lead to people trying to reassign their gender, people are now trying to define new and bizarre genders out of thin air. This has led to all kinds of confusion over pronoun usage to the point that there are probably more possible options for pronouns than anyone can possibly name. After all, the list of possible genders constantly changes with new ones being added on a regular basis. What's worse is that the some of the most vulnerable members of society, e.g., children, have been given puberty blockers and gender reassignment surgeries. 

There is no small irony that when marriage can mean anything, it now means nothing, i.e., some believe it can be whatever you want it to be to suite your sinful proclivities. When gender can mean anything, it now means nothing, i.e., whatever you want it to be in order to be true to yourself.

Where we are today, didn't appear out of nowhere. Ideas have consequences. Sometimes the fruit of those consequences do not appear and are not reaped until the next and following generations. Therefore, getting to the root of the problem requires rediscovering biblical truth. Our times require us to know and understand how God designed the world and how he intends it to function. Furthermore, by tracing ideas back to their source or seed form, we can more clearly analyze them in light of the Bible to address the underlying sinful thoughts that led to current sinful manifestations. Until we expose ourselves to the Bible in that way, we can never hope to truly address the ills of our time.

So how does that relate to the early chapters of Genesis? Genesis 1-2 describes the Almighty God as creating the world from nothing. He, through His power and infinite wisdom, formed everything by the spoken word whereby the Son carried out his Father's command through the power of the Holy Spirit. As Creator, he made everything perfectly. There was no sin and no evil. It was very good. God made man and woman as only two distinct sexes, equal as image bearers of God yet with distinct natures and characteristics for obeying his mandates to exercise dominion. God instituted the marriage between one man and one woman for life. Each family unit is a building block in fulfilling the mandate to exercise dominion.

Because of sin, death entered -- not through some evolutionary process of repetitive struggle and "refinement" that violates the Second Law of Thermodynamics. Man's sin has subjected all creation to travail, groaning, and death. But God, who is rich in mercy, promised the deliverer through the woman's offspring who would deliver us from the deceitfulness of Satan and the bondage of our wills. God presents himself as the just judge of sinners while also being the merciful deliverer.

In Genesis 4 we learn that the object of our worship is either the idol of self or the only true God of Scripture. Individuals either believe self or believe God. Only those that seek after God are those that demonstrate they have a knowledge of him. Genesis 6 demonstrates that it didn't take long for the post-Fall world to become a monolithic culture of debauchery and licentiousness. Thankfully, God's gracious nature extended grace to Noah and delivered a remnant while destroying the rest of man in a global flood. The flood highlights again that God is just and the justifier of those that trust in him for deliverance. The ark foreshadows the ark of safety that is only in Christ whereby he propitiated God's wrath and delivers every sinner that repents and trusts in him for deliverance.

All that to say this: if we took the first part of Genesis more seriously, we would know at least the following: [^Add-Genesis-1]

* God created everything from nothing
* God created man and woman
  * As equals as image bearers
  * Only two distinct sexes with overlapping natures and characteristics while at the same time unique natures and characteristics of each sex
* God's creation was very good -- nothing about it was bad
* God created marriage between one man and one woman for life
* Marriage is a foundational building block of society. The command to exercise dominion and replenish the earth was for the married couple, Adam and Eve, and for their offspring
* Work is good and commanded by God
* Rest is good and is a gift given by God for spending dedicated time to enjoy him
* Sin significantly marred God's good creation
* Death is inevitable for everyone because all have sinned, therefore, man needs to be saved
* God is just and merciful
* God promises the deliverer
* We are worshippers at heart. The question is whether it is false idol worship or the true worship of God
* Those that are righteous seek God
* We are depraved. Left to ourselves, society unravels. Sin destroys families, societies, and the world. But God's grace brings hope and deliverance
* Only those that repudiate self-trust and run to God's appointed means of deliverance will be delivered from the wrath to come

How much of the core issues of our time would be diagnosed and addressed if we understood and believed the opening chapters of Genesis at face value? How can one understand the gospel and appreciate it without knowing and understanding [Genesis 1:1-9:17](https://ref.ly/Genesis%201.1%E2%80%939.17;esv?t=biblia)?

[^Add-Genesis-1]: Much more could be written about this &mdash; at least a book. This is not intended to be exhaustive. It is to say that the current state of the West is in large part because of its ignorance, neglect, and rejection of the early part of Genesis. The way forward is to recover an understanding of how God ordered and created his world by studying and contemplating these early chapters of the Bible. Doing so will help us understand reality as God intended. Next, we must repent of the ways we have lived incongruent to that reality followed by trusting in Christ, the Redeemer and Sanctifier of our souls to save and to conform us into his likeness.
