# Salvation Testimony of Aaron Bauman

God, in his kind providence, saved my parents when I was about eleven years old. As God transformed my parents, He graciously worked on my heart. My Dad regularly led our family in devotions. He took us to church pretty much whenever the doors were open. At an evangelistic service, the Lord deeply convicted me that I was a sinner and condemned before God. I responded to this knowledge as best I knew. I felt deep shame about my sin, however, I did not understand the sufficiency of Christ's substitutionary sacrifice. Thankfully, the Lord granted me a hunger for his Word and prayer and a desire to follow my Dad's leadership.

As the Lord opened the Scriptures to me, my conscience grew in its awareness of my desperate sinfulness. Even my motivations were corrupted by selfish pride and ambition. I came to understand that the key to salvation was faith in Christ. I embraced faith in Christ and was baptized in a country stream. But because I believed that I could lose my salvation, I had little assurance of being able to stand before the Lord without being condemned. God's word kept revealing my sin. The Lord didn't allow my conscience to rest. Even "small" sins were still sins that demanded God's righteous justice. After all, it only took one bite from the fruit of the forbidden tree for Adam and Eve to be banished from the Garden and to plunge the world into despair. What hope was there for me?

 I resolved to try harder. If only I was more faithful to walk worthy of the calling to which God had called me. But even that did not lead to peace and assurance. By continually reading the Bible, the Lord kept showing me how sinful I was even with all my efforts to walk as he commanded. Occasionally, the Lord kindly gave me glimpses that I needed to walk by faith. But even my faith was fickle. I kept missing that salvation is of the Lord.

As I continued pursuing to know the Lord, he kindly brought my future wife into my life. Shortly after marrying Carol, the Lord warmed my heart with the knowledge that Christ is the one that atones for sinners. This brought peace. But that didn't last because I still believed that I could lose my salvation.

While my family grew, the Lord impressed on me the tremendous need to lead my children in God's ways. This kept me seeking for a church that honored God's Word. I wanted a place that taught us to believe and obey all that God promised and commanded. It was through the preaching and teaching of God's word that I came to believe that whom God saves, he secures. He gives his Spirit to each child of his as a guarantee of eternal salvation. Since God keeps his promises, I no longer needed to wonder whether my salvation was secure. He would preserve His own.

Even though I was armed with this new confidence about God securing me, the Lord would still confront me with my sinfulness. This caused me to begin to wonder if I was even a child of God. I slipped into old ruts of trying harder to "prove" that I was a Christian. This led to periods of greater attempts at disciplined Christian living. At other times, I became so discouraged that I would throw up my arms and wonder if I could ever make consistent progress in my sanctification.

But God in His super abundant mercy and compassion providentially directed my life into a prolonged season of tremendous difficulties. During this time, God used his Word through Bible reading and studying with others, the preaching and teaching of various men, and strained relationships with some dear Christian friends to bring me to a very low point. It was at that point, that I came to fully understand that salvation was of the Lord from start to finish. Not only does he justify sinners, he sanctifies them and he will glorify them. Since this his work from beginning to end, salvation is a sheer gift. It isn't earned. He gives the sinner faith and repentance so that he can turn from his sins and believe upon the Lord Jesus for salvation. To be justified is to be declared righteous. It is to have one's legal standing before the Lord to be forever solved. A justified sinner is forever declared righteous because his sins have been nailed to the cross. Colossians talks about our union with Christ being associated with his death and resurrection. We died in him. We also rose to newness of life in him. He exchanged our sinful robes of unrighteousness with his spotless robe of righteousness.

Because salvation is all a work of God from beginning to end, the Lord continues to give me consistent assurance that I am his child. His foreknowledge chose, convicted, effectually called, regenerated, converted, justified, adopted, sanctifies, and preserves me. I am confident that some day he will glorify me to the praise of the glory of his grace.
