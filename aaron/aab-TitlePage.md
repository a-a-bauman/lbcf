# The Doctrinal Statement of Aaron A. Bauman

## Based on The 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith

_with annotations and clarifications by Aaron Bauman_

---

I affirm all the chapters of _The 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith_ **with** the annotations, clarifications, and addendum I have added. I do so understanding that this Confession is not a perfect nor inspired document. I believe it is a faithful summary of biblical doctrine. Where I have added annotations or clarifications, I have done so to ensure that my doctrinal statement is as accurate as possible according to Scripture.

Aaron A. Bauman, $date$
