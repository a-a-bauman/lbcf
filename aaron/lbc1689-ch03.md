# Chapter 3 — Of God's Decree

## Paragraph 1

God hath decreed in himself, from all eternity, by the most wise and holy counsel of His own will, freely and unchangeably, all things, whatsoever comes to pass;[^03-1] yet so as thereby is God neither the author of sin nor hath fellowship with any therein;[^03-2] nor is violence offered to the will of the creature, nor yet is the liberty or contingency of second causes taken away, but rather established;[^03-3] in which appears His wisdom in disposing all things, and power and faithfulness in accomplishing His decree.[^03-4]

## Paragraph 2

Although God knoweth whatsoever may or can come to pass, upon all supposed conditions,[^03-5] yet hath He not decreed anything, because He foresaw it as future, or as that which would come to pass upon such conditions.[^03-6]

## Paragraph 3

By the decree of God, for the manifestation of His glory, some men and angels are predestinated, or foreordained to eternal life through Jesus Christ,[^03-7] to the praise of His glorious grace;[^03-8] others being left to act in their sin to their just condemnation, to the praise of His glorious justice.[^03-9]

## Paragraph 4

These angels and men thus predestinated and foreordained, are particularly and unchangeably designed, and their number so certain and definite, that it cannot be either increased or diminished. [^03-10]

_aab_: A clarifying comment on this chapter.

I believe that God decrees the elect to be saved while at the same time holding people responsible to respond to the call of the gospel to repent from their sins and believe in Christ alone for salvation. While the sovereignty of God in salvation seems to contradict the responsibility and the will of individuals to respond, in the mind of God, no contradiction exists. We are limited in our understanding of God and his ways. He is not. As created beings, we are called to believe all that the scriptures teach, even when we cannot harmonize or reconcile that which seems contradictory. Deuteronomy 29:29 teaches that the secret things of the Lord belong to him. Therefore, I believe that every person elected by God's will be saved according to God's sovereign decrees. I also believe that all men everywhere are responsible for their choices, including whether they do or do not respond to the gospel.
