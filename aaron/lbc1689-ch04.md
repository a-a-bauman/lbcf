# Chapter 4 — Of Creation

## Paragraph 1

In the beginning it pleased God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit,[^04-1] for the manifestation of the glory of His eternal power,[^04-2] wisdom, and goodness, to create or make the world, and all things therein, whether visible or invisible, in the space of six days, and all very good.[^04-3]

[^04-1]: [John 1:2–3](https://ref.ly/John%201.2%E2%80%933;esv?t=biblia); [Heb. 1:2](https://ref.ly/Heb.%201.2;esv?t=biblia); [Job 26:13](https://ref.ly/Job%2026.13;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-2]: [Rom. 1:20](https://ref.ly/Rom.%201.20;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-3]: [Col. 1:16](https://ref.ly/Col.%201.16;esv?t=biblia); [Gen. 1:31](https://ref.ly/Gen.%201.31;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 2

After God had made all other creatures, He created man, male and female,[^04-4] with reasonable and immortal souls,[^04-5] rendering them fit unto that life to God for which they were created; being made after the image of God, in knowledge, righteousness, and true holiness;[^04-6] having the law of God written in their hearts,[^04-7] and power to fulfill it, and yet under a possibility of transgressing, being left to the liberty of their own will, which was subject to change.[^04-8]

[^04-4]: [Gen. 1:27](https://ref.ly/Gen.%201.27;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-5]: [Gen. 2:7](https://ref.ly/Gen.%202.7;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-6]: [Eccles. 7:29](https://ref.ly/Eccles.%207.29;esv?t=biblia); [Gen. 1:26](https://ref.ly/Gen.%201.26;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-7]: [Rom. 2:14–15](https://ref.ly/Rom.%202.14%E2%80%9315;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-8]: [Gen. 3:6](https://ref.ly/Gen.%203.6;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 3

Besides the law written in their hearts, they received a command not to eat of the tree of knowledge of good and evil,[^04-9] which while they kept, they were happy in their communion with God, and had dominion over the creatures.[^04-10]

[^04-9]: [Gen. 2:17](https://ref.ly/Gen.%202.17;esv?t=biblia)  
[^04-10]: [Gen. 1:26](https://ref.ly/Gen.%201.26;esv?t=biblia),[28](https://ref.ly/Gen%201.28;esv?t=biblia)
