# Of Adoption — The 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith

# Chapter 12

## Paragraph 1

All those that are justified, God conferred, in and for the sake of his only Son Jesus Christ, to make partakers of the grace of adoption,[^12-1] by which they are taken into the number, and enjoy the liberties and privileges of the children of God,[^12-2] have his name put on them,[^12-3] receive the spirit of adoption,[^12-4] have access to the throne of grace with boldness, are enabled to cry Abba, Father,[^12-5] are pitied,[^12-6] protected,[^12-7] provided for,[^12-8] and chastened by him as by a Father,[^12-9] yet never cast off,[^12-10] but sealed to the day of redemption,[^12-11] and inherit the promises as heirs of everlasting salvation.[^12-12]
