# Chapter 22 — Of Religious Worship and the Sabbath Day

## Paragraph 1

The light of nature shows that there is a God, who has lordship and sovereignty over all; is just, good and does good to all; and is therefore to be feared, loved, praised, called upon, trusted in, and served, with all the heart and all the soul, and with all the might.[^22-1] But the acceptable way of worshipping the true God, is instituted by himself,[^22-2] and so limited by his own revealed will, that he may not be worshipped according to the imagination and devices of men, nor the suggestions of Satan, under any visible representations, or any other way not prescribed in the Holy Scriptures.[^22-3]

[^22-1]: [Jer. 10:7](https://ref.ly/Jer.%2010.7;esv?t=biblia); [Mark 12:33](https://ref.ly/Mark%2012.33;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-2]: [Deut. 12:32](https://ref.ly/Deut.%2012.32;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-3]: [Exod. 20:4–6](https://ref.ly/Exod.%2020.4%E2%80%936;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 2

Religious worship is to be given to God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and to him alone;[^22-4] not to angels, saints, or any other creatures;[^22-5] and since the fall, not without a mediator,[^22-6] nor in the mediation of any other but Christ alone.[^22-7]

[^22-4]: [Matt. 4:9–10](https://ref.ly/Matt.%204.9%E2%80%9310;esv?t=biblia); [John 6:23](https://ref.ly/John%206.23;esv?t=biblia); [Matt. 28:19](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2028.19;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-5]: [Rom. 1:25](https://ref.ly/Rom.%201.25;esv?t=biblia); [Col. 2:18](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.18;esv?t=biblia); [Rev. 19:10](https://ref.ly/Rev.%2019.10;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-6]: [John 14:6](https://ref.ly/John%2014.6;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-7]: [1 Tim. 2:5](https://ref.ly/1%20Tim.%202.5;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 3

Prayer, with thanksgiving, being one part of natural worship, is by God required of all men.[^22-8] But that it may be accepted, it is to be made in the name of the Son,[^22-9] by the help of the Spirit,[^22-10] according to his will;[^22-11] with understanding, reverence, humility, fervency, faith, love, and perseverance; and when with others, in a known tongue.[^22-12]

[^22-8]: [Ps. 95:1–7](https://ref.ly/Ps.%2095.1%E2%80%937;esv?t=biblia), [65:2](https://ref.ly/Ps%2065.2;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-9]: [John 14:13–14](https://ref.ly/John%2014.13%E2%80%9314;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-10]: [Rom. 8:26](https://ref.ly/Rom.%208.26;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-11]: [1 John 5:14](https://ref.ly/1%20John%205.14;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-12]: [1 Cor. 14:16–17](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2014.16%E2%80%9317;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 4

Prayer is to be made for things lawful, and for all sorts of men living, or that shall live hereafter;[^22-13] but not for the dead,[^22-14] nor for those of whom it may be known that they have sinned the sin unto death.[^22-15]

[^22-13]: [1 Tim. 2:1–2](https://ref.ly/1%20Tim.%202.1%E2%80%932;esv?t=biblia); [2 Sam. 7:29](https://ref.ly/2%20Sam.%207.29;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-14]: [2 Sam. 12:21–23](https://ref.ly/2%20Sam.%2012.21%E2%80%9323;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-15]: [1 John 5:16](https://ref.ly/1%20John%205.16;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 5

The reading of the Scriptures,[^22-16] preaching, and hearing the Word of God,[^22-17] teaching and admonishing one another in psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing with grace in our hearts to the Lord;[^22-18] as also the administration of baptism,[^22-19] and the Lord's supper,[^22-20] are all parts of religious worship of God, to be performed in obedience to him, with understanding, faith, reverence, and godly fear; moreover, solemn humiliation, with fastings,[^22-21] and thanksgivings, upon special occasions, ought to be used in an holy and religious manner.[^22-22]

## Paragraph 6

Neither prayer nor any other part of religious worship, is now under the gospel, tied unto, or made more acceptable by any place in which it is performed, or towards which it is directed; but God is to be worshipped everywhere in spirit and in truth;[^22-23] as in private families[^22-24] daily,[^22-25] and in secret each one by himself;[^22-26] so more solemnly in the public assemblies, which are not carelessly nor wilfully to be neglected or forsaken, when God by his word or providence calls thereunto.[^22-27]

[^22-23]: [John 4:21](https://ref.ly/John%204.21;esv?t=biblia); [Mal. 1:11](https://ref.ly/Mal.%201.11;esv?t=biblia); [1 Tim. 2:8](https://ref.ly/1%20Tim.%202.8;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-24]: [Acts 10:2](https://ref.ly/Acts%2010.2;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-25]: [Matt. 6:11](https://ref.ly/Matt.%206.11;esv?t=biblia); [Ps. 55:17](https://ref.ly/Ps.%2055.17;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-26]: [Matt. 6:6](https://ref.ly/Matt.%206.6;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-27]: [Heb. 10:25](https://ref.ly/Heb.%2010.25;esv?t=biblia); [Acts 2:42](https://ref.ly/Acts%202.42;esv?t=biblia)  

## Paragraph 7

As it is the ==law of nature==, that in general a proportion of time, by God's appointment, be set apart for the worship of God, so by his Word, in a positive moral, and perpetual commandment, binding all men, in all ages, he has particularly appointed one day in seven for a sabbath to be kept holy unto him,[^22-28] which ==from the beginning of the world== [^aab-22-1] to the resurrection of Christ was the last day of the week, and from the resurrection of Christ was changed into the first day of the week, which is called the Lord's Day:[^22-29] and is to be continued to the end of the world as the Christian Sabbath, the observation of the last day of the week being abolished.[^aab-22-2]

[^22-28]: [Exod. 20:8](https://ref.ly/Exod.%2020.8;esv?t=biblia)  
[^aab-22-1]: _aab_ I believe a day of rest and worship is baked into the very foundation of creation. [Genesis 2:1-3](https://ref.ly/Genesis.%202.1-3). God confirms that when giving the Sabbath commandment in [Exodus 20:11](https://ref.ly/Exodus%2020.11;esv?t=biblia). When correcting false views about the Sabbath commandment, Jesus took his accusers all the way back to creation to show to them that God gave the Sabbath as a gift to man, not the other way around. Additionally, the effects of the Fall ushered in sweeping turmoil that touches every aspect of life including broken relationships, difficulty in providing for basic needs, and eventually death. Before the Fall, God gave the gift of the Sabbath to Adam and Even. After the Fall, how much more did they need the gift of that day to recalibrate their minds toward the Savior who would rescue them?
[^22-29]: [1 Cor. 16:1–2](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2016.1%E2%80%932;esv?t=biblia); [Acts 20:7](https://ref.ly/Acts%2020.7;esv?t=biblia); [Rev. 1:10](https://ref.ly/Rev.%201.10;esv?t=biblia)
[^aab-22-2]: _aab_ [Hebrews 4](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%204;esv?t=biblia) contains a beautiful interplay between the seventh day of creation week as rest and entering Christ through faith for genuine rest to the soul. Verses 9-10 seem to point to not just the rest that believers in Christ currently enjoy but that they will enjoy for all eternity. This is underscored in verse 10 with the warning of not entering that rest. This corresponds to Jesus invitating weary sinners to enter salvation's rest. [Matthew 11:28-30](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2011.28-30;esv?t=biblia).

## Paragraph 8

The sabbath is then kept holy unto the Lord, when men, after a due preparing of their hearts, and ordering their common affairs aforehand, do not only observe a holy rest all day, from ==their own works, words and thoughts, about their worldly employment and recreations,[^22-30] but are also taken up the whole time in the public and private exercises of his worship, and in the duties of necessity and mercy.==[^22-31][^aab-22-3]

[^22-30]: [Isa. 58:13](https://ref.ly/Isa.%2058.13;esv?t=biblia); [Neh. 13:15–22](https://ref.ly/Neh.%2013.15%E2%80%9322;esv?t=biblia)  
[^22-31]: [Matt. 12:1–13](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2012.1%E2%80%9313;esv?t=biblia)
[^aab-22-3]: _aab_ I generally agree with this statement. I don't believe that honoring the Lord's day means that all work stops. Otherwise, one would not get out of bed. The same applies to words and thoughts. The key is that the day is different from all others. When God made the seventh day holy, he set apart that day as distinct from the others. Considering the Lord's Day as different from all the rest is key. The other key is to recognize that Jesus is our eternal rest which he secured for us on the cross and through the resurrection on the _first day_ of the week. Forever, we look to him for that perfect rest that our souls so desperately need. The Lord's Day is a gift from God to worship Christ and seek his kingdom through ministering to others. As for the specifics of what that looks like, there should be a certain amount of allowance for the priesthood of each believer and household while also being sensitive to obey the command to not neglect gathering with God's people. [Hebrews 10:19-25](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2010.19-25;esv?t=biblia)
