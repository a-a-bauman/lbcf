# Chapter 28 — Of Baptism and the Lord's Supper

## Paragraph 1

Baptism and the Lord's Supper are ordinances of positive and sovereign institution, appointed by the Lord Jesus, the only lawgiver, to be continued in his church to the end of the world.[^28-1]

[^28-1]: [Matt. 28:19–20](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2028.19%E2%80%9320;esv?t=biblia); [1 Cor. 11:26](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2011.26;esv?t=biblia)

## Paragraph 2

These holy appointments are to be administered by those only who are qualified and thereunto called[^aab-28-1], according to the commission of Christ.[^28-2]

[^aab-28-1]: I believe it is allowable and commendable for the head of a household to baptize his children or wife provided he is a believer, a member of the church, and the baptism is overseen by an elder.
[^28-2]: [Matt. 28:19](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2028.19;esv?t=biblia); [1 Cor. 4:1](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%204.1;esv?t=biblia)

I, Aaron Bauman, affirm all of this chapter on 2025-08-24 with the comment on Paragraph 2.
